CHINA STEP BY STEP

Privacy Policy

An independent, offline-first travel guide for international visitors to China. No accounts, advertising or developer-operated data servers.

Effective and last updated: 29 July 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Scope

This policy applies to the iOS app “China Step by Step” (bundle identifier: com.chinastepbystep.guide).

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores only your selected language, completed guide step identifiers and saved guide identifiers in local iOS preferences. This restores your choices and progress. The information remains on your device and is not uploaded to us.

3. Data we do not collect

The current version has no user account, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking technology or developer-operated server. We do not collect or receive names, email addresses, passport or payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, health information, device identifiers or usage history.

4. Permissions

The current version does not request location, camera, photo-library, microphone, contacts, Bluetooth or health-data access. It does not ask you to enter passport or bank-card details inside the guide.

5. External apps, websites and calls

Links may open Apple’s App Store, official government pages, or third-party apps and websites. Telephone buttons open the iPhone dialler and require your confirmation before calling. Apple, your carrier and each third party process information under their own policies. Verify the app, publisher, address, payee and number before continuing.

6. Retention and deletion

Local preferences remain until you clear progress or delete the app. The Trip tab can remove saved guides and completion history. Deleting the app removes its local app data according to iOS behaviour. Because we never receive this data, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely.

7. Children, security and tracking

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Local data is protected by the standard iOS app sandbox. We do not sell personal information, create advertising profiles or track users across apps or websites.

8. Independence and disclaimer

This is not an official product of, or endorsed by, Apple, WeChat, Alipay, China Railway 12306, DiDi, Meituan, Dianping or any government authority. Third-party names and trade marks belong to their owners. Travel rules and services change; verify important decisions with official sources.

9. Changes and contact

If features, permissions or data practices change, we will revise this policy before the relevant release. For privacy questions, contact the developer through the developer’s GitHub profile.

中文隐私说明

“China Step by Step”是一款面向外国游客的独立中国旅行指南。当前版本没有账号、广告、分析追踪或开发者自有服务器。

本机数据

应用只在设备上保存语言选择、已完成步骤编号和收藏指南编号，用于恢复使用状态。本应用不会将这些信息上传给开发者。

不收集的数据与权限

本应用不收集姓名、邮箱、护照、银行卡、精确位置、联系人、照片、录音、健康信息、设备标识或使用历史；当前版本也不申请定位、相机、照片、麦克风、通讯录、蓝牙或健康权限。

外部服务与电话

按钮可能打开 App Store、政府网站或第三方服务。电话按钮只打开 iPhone 拨号界面，仍需用户确认。离开应用后的数据处理受 Apple、运营商及相应第三方政策约束。

删除与联系

可在“行程”页清除收藏和完成记录；卸载应用也会移除其本地数据。如对隐私有疑问，可通过开发者 GitHub 主页联系。
